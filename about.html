Redefining Trading Education in Zambia

Our journey began in 2019 as Topnotch Traders Capital. We observed an acute void in the market: retail participants were misled by retail indicators and deceptive guru promises, leading to a 90%+ failure rate.

In 2023, with institutional partnerships and proprietary algorithmic research, we officially scaled into EdgeGrid Traders Capital. We provide students with direct trading desk experience, institutional liquidity concepts (SMC/IPDA), and funded capital.
